Export Documentation

Export Documentation for Smooth Global Trade

We prepare essential export documents to support customs clearance, regulatory compliance, and smooth international trade operations.

Commercial Invoice

Official invoice detailing products, quantities, and transaction value.

Packing List

Detailed packaging information for logistics and customs purposes.

Certificate of Origin

Verification of product origin issued according to export regulations.

Phytosanitary Certificate

Available when required for agricultural and plant-based products.

Bill of Lading

Shipping documentation provided through freight and logistics partners.

Import Requirement Support

Support for additional certificates and documentation based on destination market regulations and importer requirements.

All export documents are prepared according to product specifications, destination country regulations, and importer requirements.

Whether you manage your own logistics network or require end-to-port delivery, we can accommodate different trade terms to align with your procurement and shipping preferences.

CIF Available

Includes freight and insurance coverage up to the destination port.

CFR

CFR Available

EXW

EXW Available
