About Our Company

About Nanda Food Export

Learn more about Nanda Food Export, our company background, business values, and how we support international buyers through reliable sourcing, export expertise, and long-term partnerships.

What is Nanda Food Export?

Nanda Food Export is the international business division of PT. Nanda Food And Agriculture, an Indonesian company focused on supplying premium food and agricultural products to global markets. We work with international importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and private label brands by providing reliable sourcing, export coordination, and long-term business support. Our objective is not only to deliver quality products, but also to build sustainable partnerships through professionalism, transparency, and responsive customer service.

Where is Nanda Food Export located?

Our company is based in Aceh Besar, Aceh Province, Indonesia, a region well known for its rich agricultural resources and food production. This strategic location allows us to collaborate closely with local producers and manufacturing partners while ensuring efficient product sourcing and export preparation. From Indonesia, we serve customers worldwide by supporting international shipments and helping buyers access authentic Indonesian food products with confidence.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We proudly support a wide variety of business partners, including food importers, distributors, wholesalers, supermarkets, retail chains, private label companies, hospitality businesses, and food service providers. Whether you are launching a new food brand or expanding an existing product portfolio, our team is ready to provide sourcing, export guidance, and customized business solutions tailored to your market requirements and long-term commercial goals.

Why should international buyers choose Nanda Food Export?

International buyers choose Nanda Food Export because we combine high-quality Indonesian products with dependable export services and responsive communication. We focus on building long-term business relationships rather than one-time transactions. Our commitment includes consistent product quality, flexible business solutions, private label opportunities, export documentation support, and reliable coordination from inquiry through shipment. We strive to become a trusted sourcing partner that helps our customers grow successfully in their respective markets.

Products

Products & Manufacturing

Explore our Indonesian food products, manufacturing capabilities, private label services, and production flexibility designed to support importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and food businesses worldwide.

What products does Nanda Food Export offer?

Nanda Food Export supplies a diverse range of Indonesian food and agricultural products for international markets. Our portfolio includes premium banana chips, cassava chips, cookies, and selected agricultural commodities sourced from trusted partners across Indonesia. We continuously expand our product selection to meet changing market demand while maintaining consistent quality and export readiness. Whether you are looking for packaged consumer products or agricultural commodities, our team can recommend suitable solutions based on your target market and business model.

Are your products made from natural Indonesian ingredients?

Yes. Our products are produced using carefully selected Indonesian agricultural ingredients sourced from trusted suppliers and farming communities. Every product is prepared with attention to quality, consistency, and food safety to ensure it meets buyer expectations. We focus on preserving the authentic taste and character of Indonesian food while maintaining production standards suitable for international distribution. Quality control is applied throughout the sourcing and production process to help ensure every shipment delivers consistent results.

Do you provide OEM and Private Label services?

Yes. We offer OEM manufacturing and private label solutions for importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and companies developing their own food brands. Depending on the project, we can support customized packaging, product branding, labeling, and product presentation to match your business identity. Our objective is to help partners introduce Indonesian food products under their own brand while maintaining product quality, attractive packaging, and reliable export support throughout the production process.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

The minimum order quantity depends on several factors, including the product category, packaging specifications, private label requirements, and destination country. Some products may have a standard MOQ, while customized production often requires a different quantity to achieve efficient manufacturing and export preparation. Our sales team will gladly discuss your purchasing plans and recommend the most suitable order quantity based on your business objectives, market demand, and shipping preferences.

Export Services

Export & International Shipping

Understand how we manage international exports, shipping coordination, export documentation, and end-to-end logistics support to help businesses import Indonesian food products with confidence.

Which countries does Nanda Food Export serve?

Nanda Food Export serves buyers from various international markets, including Asia, the Middle East, Europe, Africa, Oceania, and the Americas. We continuously expand our global network by working with importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and food service companies seeking reliable Indonesian food products. If your country is not listed on our website, our team will be happy to evaluate your destination and discuss available export options based on local import regulations and shipping accessibility.

What export documents do you provide?

We provide the standard commercial documents required for international trade, including the Commercial Invoice and Packing List. Depending on the destination country, product category, and customer requirements, additional export documentation may also be prepared in accordance with applicable regulations. Our team coordinates the documentation process carefully to help ensure smooth customs clearance and efficient shipment handling, allowing buyers to receive their orders with confidence.

Can you assist first-time importers?

Absolutely. We understand that many businesses are importing Indonesian products for the first time. Our team provides guidance throughout the export process, from selecting suitable products and discussing packaging requirements to preparing quotations, coordinating production, arranging export documentation, and organizing shipment. We aim to make the purchasing process straightforward and transparent, enabling new buyers to establish international sourcing with greater confidence and reduced complexity.

How does the export process work?

Our export process begins with your inquiry, where we discuss product specifications, quantities, packaging preferences, and destination requirements. After confirming the quotation, production and quality control are carried out before products are packed according to export standards. We then prepare the necessary export documentation and coordinate international shipping with the selected logistics partners. Throughout the process, we maintain clear communication to keep buyers informed until their shipment is dispatched.

Contact & Partnership

Contact & Business Partnership

Get answers about quotations, business inquiries, response times, and partnership opportunities. Our team is ready to support importers and distributors with professional guidance from the first inquiry to long-term cooperation.

How can I request a quotation?

Requesting a quotation is simple. You can contact Nanda Food Export through our Contact Form, email, or WhatsApp. To help us prepare an accurate quotation, please include the product you are interested in, the estimated order quantity, your destination country, preferred packaging, and any special requirements. Our team will review your inquiry and provide a detailed quotation along with the necessary product and export information.

What information should I prepare before contacting your team?

To ensure an efficient consultation, we recommend preparing several important details before contacting us. These include your company name, destination country, products of interest, estimated order quantity, preferred packaging type, and whether you require OEM or private label services. Providing complete information enables our team to understand your business needs and recommend the most suitable products and export solutions.

How long does it take to receive a response?

Our team aims to respond to all business inquiries as promptly as possible during normal business hours. Response times may vary depending on the complexity of your request and local public holidays, but we are committed to providing clear, professional, and timely communication. If additional information is required to prepare a quotation or discuss product specifications, our team will guide you through the next steps.

Can I become a long-term business partner?

Absolutely. We welcome long-term partnerships with importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, supermarkets, food service companies, and private label brands worldwide. Our goal is to build sustainable business relationships based on product quality, transparent communication, and dependable export support. Whether you are developing a new product line or expanding your existing business, Nanda Food Export is committed to becoming your trusted Indonesian sourcing partner.
