Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Our Products & Export Services

Learn more about our premium Indonesian snack products, export capabilities, private label solutions, and international shipping services for distributors, wholesalers, and global buyers.

Do you export Indonesian snacks worldwide?

Yes. Nanda Food Export supplies premium Indonesian snack products to international markets and supports worldwide shipping with complete export documentation.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

The minimum order quantity depends on the product type, packaging requirements, and destination country. Contact our team to receive a customized quotation based on your business needs.

Do you offer private label and custom packaging services?

Absolutely. We provide private label solutions and custom packaging options to help distributors, retailers, wholesalers, and importers build their own brand.

Are your products made from natural Indonesian ingredients?

Yes. Our products are made from carefully selected agricultural ingredients sourced from Indonesia and processed under strict quality control standards to ensure freshness, consistency, and great taste.

What export documents can you provide?

We can prepare essential export documents including commercial invoices, packing lists, and other supporting documents required for international trade and customs clearance.

How can I request a quotation or become a business partner?

Simply contact us via WhatsApp or email and share your product requirements, estimated order quantity, destination country, and packaging preferences. Our team will respond with a tailored quotation and partnership proposal.
